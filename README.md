# Gloop Drop

A physics merge puzzle for iPhone.

Drop orbs into the jar. Two of the same colour merge into a bigger one. Chain
merges together for a bigger multiplier, and don't let the jar overflow past
the line.

- **Support:** iamtommetcalfe@gmail.com

## Common questions

**Does it collect any data?**
No. Your best score and music preference are stored on your device and never
transmitted. There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no tracking.

**Do I need Game Center?**
No. Leaderboards are optional — decline the sign-in and the game plays exactly
the same.

**How do I turn the music off?**
Tap the ♪ button at the bottom of the screen. The setting is remembered.

**Something's broken.**
Email the address above with your device model and iOS version.

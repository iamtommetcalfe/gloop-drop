Privacy Policy — Gloop Drop

Last updated: 4 September 2026

The short version

Gloop Drop collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. Nothing you do in the game is sent to me, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

What the app stores

Two things are saved on your device and nowhere else:

  • Your best score
  • Whether you have the music turned on or off

Both live in the app's own storage on your device. Deleting the app removes them. Neither is transmitted anywhere.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your score is submitted to an Apple leaderboard when a run ends, and the app can display that leaderboard. This is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The app never sees your Apple Account details, and if you are not signed in to Game Center the feature is simply unavailable — the game plays exactly the same.

Children

The app is rated suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
